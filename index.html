About InclusiveTech

Breaking Barriers. Building Opportunities.

Inclusive technology helps create workplaces where people with different abilities can participate and contribute. Accessible digital tools can make everyday work tasks more manageable and communication more effective.

An inclusive workplace recognizes individual needs, respects differences, and provides fair opportunities for employees to succeed.

Our Mission

To promote awareness of accessible technology and inclusive practices in the workplace.

Our Vision

A future where everyone has fair opportunities to participate and succeed at work.

Digital Accessibility

Technology That Makes a Difference

Accessible design and assistive tools can help people interact with digital content in different ways.

▤

Screen Readers

Read aloud text and describe digital interface elements to help blind and low-vision users navigate websites and documents.

🎙

Voice Recognition

Allows users to dictate text and control supported computer functions using spoken commands.

CC

Captions & Transcripts

Provide text alternatives for spoken content, helping people follow videos, meetings, and training.

⌨

Keyboard Navigation

Enables people to navigate supported digital interfaces without relying on a mouse.

◐

Display & Text Options

Text resizing, clear contrast, and other display options can make digital information easier to read.

⌘

Adaptive Input Devices

Alternative keyboards, switches, and pointing devices can support different motor and input needs.
