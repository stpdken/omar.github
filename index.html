01 / 05

Accessibility Technologies

Accessibility technologies are features built into digital systems that help people with different abilities access and interact with information.

🔊

Screen Readers

Converts text and interface information into spoken output.

⌨️

Keyboard Navigation

Allows users to navigate digital interfaces without relying on a mouse.

🔍

Text & Display Tools

Adjustable text size, contrast, and display settings can improve readability.

02 / 05

Assistive Technologies

Assistive technology includes devices and software that help people perform tasks and interact with workplace technology.

🎙️

Voice Recognition

Allows users to control computers and create text using spoken commands.

🖱️

Alternative Input Devices

Specialized devices can provide different ways to control a computer.

🦾

Adaptive Tools

Technology can be adapted to support individual workplace needs.

03 / 05

Digital Inclusion

Digital inclusion means ensuring that people can access and meaningfully use digital technologies, information, and workplace resources.

🌐

Internet Access

Employees need reliable access to digital workplace resources.

📱

Digital Tools

Workplace platforms should be usable by people with different abilities.

🤝

Digital Participation

Everyone should have opportunities to communicate and collaborate online.

04 / 05

Accessible Content

Digital content should be designed so that users can understand and navigate information regardless of their abilities.

📝

Readable Text

Clear fonts, spacing, headings, and language improve readability.

🖼️

Alternative Text

Descriptions help users understand important information in images.

🎬

Captions

Captions provide access to spoken information in videos.

05 / 05

Equal Access to Technology

An inclusive workplace provides employees with fair opportunities to access technology and participate in digital work.

⚖️

Fair Opportunity

Technology should not create unnecessary barriers to workplace participation.

💡

Flexible Solutions

Different employees may benefit from different technological solutions.

❤️

Inclusive Culture

Accessibility becomes stronger when inclusion is part of workplace culture.

WHY IT MATTERS

Building a workplace without unnecessary barriers.

Inclusive technology helps employees access information, communicate, collaborate, and complete their responsibilities. Accessibility should be considered when selecting and designing digital workplace tools.
